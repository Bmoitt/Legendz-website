Our story

Cooked with love,
served with pride.

For years, Tandeka and Wayne poured their love for cooking into every holiday — turning each gathering into a feast fit for royalty. Now they're bringing that same warmth and tradition to the streets.

Legendz is proudly Caribbean flavors with a Southern twist: rich jerk chicken, slow-stewed goat, golden fried plantains, and desserts that taste like home. Every plate tells a story.
